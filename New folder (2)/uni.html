Why University of Peshawar

75 Years of Building Pakistan's Future

Legacy of Academic Excellence

As KPK's flagship university, UoP has shaped the region's intellectual, cultural, and political landscape for three-quarters of a century. Our graduates lead government, industry, and academia across Pakistan and beyond.

  • Continuously ranked by HEC among Pakistan's top universities
  • 75 years of unbroken academic tradition and innovation
  • 3,500+ distinguished alumni in key roles worldwide

World-Class Research Infrastructure

Our 40+ specialized research centers, modern laboratories, and a central library housing over 200,000 volumes create an environment where breakthrough research happens every day.

  • State-of-the-art laboratories across all faculties
  • Digital library with international journal access
  • 120+ active international research collaborations

Scholarships & Financial Support

We believe talent should never be held back by financial barriers. UoP offers comprehensive scholarship programs to ensure the brightest students from across Pakistan can access world-class education.

  • Merit-based scholarships for top academic performers
  • HEC Needs-Based Scholarship Programme
  • On-campus employment and work-study opportunities
